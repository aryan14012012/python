Our Engineering Services

End-to-end manufacturing solutions designed to meet the highest industry standards

Custom CNC Machining

Advanced milling and turning services handling complex multi-axis geometric specifications. Our state-of-the-art CNC machines ensure precision manufacturing with tolerances as tight as ±0.001mm. We work with various materials including steel, aluminum, titanium, and exotic alloys.

Prototyping & CAD Design

Rapid physical prototyping from digital 3D models to validate form, fit, and function. Our engineering team uses advanced CAD/CAM software to design and optimize components before production, reducing time-to-market and ensuring design perfection.

Stress & Durability Testing

In-house destructive and non-destructive testing to verify structural lifespan. We conduct comprehensive fatigue testing, tensile strength analysis, and quality inspections to ensure every component meets the highest safety and performance standards.

Precision Grinding

Surface and cylindrical grinding services for achieving exceptional surface finishes and dimensional accuracy. Ideal for components requiring tight tolerances and smooth surface textures for optimal performance.

Assembly & Integration

Complete assembly services for complex automotive systems. Our skilled technicians ensure precise fitting and integration of components, delivering ready-to-install assemblies that meet exact specifications.

Quality Assurance

Comprehensive quality control processes including CMM inspection, dimensional verification, and material certification. We maintain rigorous quality standards to deliver defect-free products consistently.
